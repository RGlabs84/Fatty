**⛤ Legendary Recipes**  
**Legendary recipes** are special recipes with special effects. The foods have higher stats and higher duration than conventional foods as well as the feasts and the potions are pretty ~~overpower~~ strong. To craft these recipes, you will need *Eldhrímnir*; a new cauldron (see New Pieces chart at the bottom). In norse mythology, Eldhrímnir is the cauldron in which Andhrímnir, the chef of the gods, prepares Sæhrímnir — a legendary beast, hunted by the einherjar — every evening.
